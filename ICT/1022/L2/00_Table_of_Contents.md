# 📚 L2: Digital Computer

### 1. 💻 [Digital Computer & Programs](?note=ICT/1022/L2/01_Digital_Computer_and_Programs.md)

### 2. 🔄 [Translators and High-Level Languages](?note=ICT/1022/L2/02_Translators_and_Languages.md)
