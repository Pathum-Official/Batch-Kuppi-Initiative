The Kitchen Analogy: Think of pipelining like 5 chefs working an assembly line. Chef 1 preps raw ingredients (Fetch), Chef 2 chops them (Decode), Chef 3 cooks on the stove (Execute), Chef 4 plates the food (Memory), and Chef 5 sends it out to the dining room (Writeback). Once the pipeline is full, a complete meal leaves the kitchen on every single clock tick.
